## Color Contrast Comparison

### BEFORE (Hard to Read ❌)
```
Background: Light Gray
Text: Medium Gray #666666
Contrast Ratio: 3.2:1 (NOT WCAG AA)
Result: Hard to read 👎
```

### AFTER (Easy to Read ✅)
```
Light Mode:
  Background: White
  Text: Dark Gray #1F2937
  Contrast Ratio: 8.6:1 ✅ (WCAG AA PASS)

Dark Mode:
  Background: Dark Gray/Black
  Text: White #FFFFFF
  Contrast Ratio: 10:1 ✅ (WCAG AAA PASS)

Result: Professional and accessible! 👍
```
